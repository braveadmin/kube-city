Three ways to build the city

The same namespace block drawn in three styles. Each image pixel is half a terminal character cell, so this is exactly the detail kube-city can draw. The images are only enlarged for viewing.

1 · Metropolis

Daylight pixel art in the spirit of SimCity 2000. Plenty of detail and easy to read at a glance.

What it costs in terminal space

The detail needs bigger tiles: 16×8 pixels instead of today's 8×4. One 4×4 block with its roads then fills about 112×48 characters, so a whole cluster no longer fits on one screen. That makes zoom part of this step:

  • Detailed: these samples, for one namespace or a big screen.
  • Overview: today's small tiles in the chosen style's colours, to see the whole city.

Not shown yet

  • Animation: buildings rising floor by floor, demolition, flickering fire and blinking beacons.
  • Namespace name badges.
  • Variety within a style. Today one workload means one building; each style can get a few shapes per kind.

Terminal fit

All three only need a terminal with true colour, which Windows Terminal has. The dark background around each scene matches a dark terminal. Toy town and Metropolis are bright scenes on that background, while Neon blends into it.

Your pick

Choose one style, or a mix, for example Metropolis buildings with Neon lighting. The chosen style becomes the default; the others can stay as a --style option if you like them.
